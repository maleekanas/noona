Security & Privacy

What we collect, why, and who can see it.

This page describes AgentPost's actual data-handling practices in plain language, written for the people using the product — not a legal team. We'll update it as the product and our certifications change; nothing here is boilerplate we don't mean.

What we collect How we use it Where it lives Your rights (GDPR) Team permissions Platform connections Certifications If something goes wrong Contact

What we collect

Only what's needed to run the product you're using:

  • Account details — name, email, and the Brands you set up.
  • Platform access tokens — issued when you connect Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and the rest, scoped to exactly the permissions you approved in that platform's connection wizard.
  • Content you create or upload — drafts, images, captions, and the posting history the agent studies to draft new ones.
  • Performance data — the metrics each connected platform returns to us (reach, engagement, clicks) and website analytics from your tracking tag.
  • Billing details — handled entirely by our payment processor. AgentPost never stores full card numbers.

How we use it

Your posting history trains the agent's drafts for your Brands only — it's never used to draft posts for anyone else's account, and it's never sold to advertisers or data brokers. Nothing publishes to a connected platform without a person approving it, either you or a teammate with permission.

Where it lives

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) between your browser, AgentPost, and the platforms you connect, and encrypted at rest in our database. Regional data residency for EU customers — keeping that data on EU infrastructure — is on our near-term roadmap; it isn't live yet, and this page will say so plainly the day it changes.

Your rights, GDPR and beyond

If you're in the EU/EEA, UK, or a region with similar protections, you can ask us to:

  • Send a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct anything that's wrong
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export your data in a portable format

Email privacy@agentpost.com — we respond within 30 days, and usually much sooner. A Data Processing Agreement is available on request for business and agency customers.

Team permissions, per Brand

Every teammate you add gets a role scoped to a specific Brand, not blanket account access:

RoleWhat they can do
OwnerFull control of the Brand, including billing and removing other members.
AdminManage platform connections, team members, and settings — no access to billing.
EditorCreate, schedule, and approve content; reply in the inbox; can't change connections or team.
Client-viewerRead-only access to reports and dashboards — no posting, no raw account data.

How we connect to platforms

AgentPost connects to Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, Pinterest, YouTube, Twitch, Google Business Profile, Threads, Bluesky, WhatsApp Business, Telegram, and Discord only through each platform's official, documented API. We don't scrape pages or rely on undocumented endpoints, and we monitor token health so a connection doesn't silently expire and break your posting schedule.

Certifications

SOC 2 Type II: in progress, not yet certified. We're publishing this page before certification on purpose — we'd rather be accurate now than quiet until there's a badge to show.

If something goes wrong

If we discover a security incident that affects your data, we'll notify affected customers without undue delay — in line with GDPR's 72-hour expectation for personal data breaches — and tell you plainly what happened, what data was involved, and what we're doing about it.

Contact

Questions about this page, a Data Processing Agreement, or anything else data-related: privacy@agentpost.com.
